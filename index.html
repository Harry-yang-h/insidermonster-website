什么是局内人What is Insider

局内人是一款面向在英华人留学生的线下活动组局 App。你可以发起一个局，也可以加入附近的局，和志同道合的人一起出门。 Insider is a group-activity app for Chinese students in the UK. Host a meetup or join one nearby, and get out with people who share your interests.

怎么用How it works

  1. 1

    发现附近的局Discover nearby

    按 1–5 公里的范围，查看正在招募的活动。Browse activities that are recruiting within 1–5 km of you.

  2. 2

    报名或发起Join or host

    在线报名付款，人数凑齐自动成团。Sign up and pay in the app; the group is confirmed once enough people join.

  3. 3

    线下见面Meet up

    到场签到，活动结束后互相评价。Check in on arrival and leave reviews after the activity.

安心组局Peace of mind

  • 人数不足自动退款Automatic refunds

    活动没凑齐人数会自动取消，费用原路退回。If an activity doesn't reach its minimum size, it's cancelled and you're refunded.

  • 信用记录Reliability score

    守约情况会记入信用分，爽约会有相应处理。Attendance is tracked in a reliability score, and no-shows have consequences.

  • 举报与客服Report and support

    遇到问题可以随时举报或联系我们。Report problems or contact us at any time.
